Girlfriend's Day · 1 August

Krimu,

Happy Girlfriend's Day, my Krimu ❤️

You're not just my girlfriend. You're my happiness, my comfort, and the most beautiful part of my life. Thank you for understanding me, supporting me, and loving me through every version of me.

When I think about us, I don't first think of one grand moment. I think of Udaipur, of us smiling with the lake behind us. I think of long drives where the road kept going and I didn't mind, because you were next to me.

I think of you asleep on my shoulder while I stayed awake, trying not to move, secretly wishing the journey would take a little longer.

I remember giving you flowers, but what stayed with me was the hug after. That is how it is with you. The plan can be lovely, the place can be beautiful, but the part I keep is always something small you did without thinking.

I love our loud laughs, but I love our quiet days just as much. The days when nothing special happens and we can simply be ourselves. You make those days matter to me.

I'm proud of you, Krimu. Of your heart, your strength, and the way you care about people. I notice more than I always know how to say. Whatever the day looks like, you'll always have me beside you.

And I hope you know that I choose you. Not only in these photographs or on days with flowers, but on the ordinary, messy, quiet days too. I choose you in all of them.

Happy Girlfriend's Day, Krimu.
I'm very lucky I get to love you.
I love you. Always.
Nayan
